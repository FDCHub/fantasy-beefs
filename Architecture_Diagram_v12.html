Lineup snapshot at acceptance — RULED, UNBUILT, NEW SCORING ENGINE
NEW BLOCK, and it's bigger than it sounds. What the app does today: _eval_beef() → _team_score_for_week() reads Matchup.home_score — Yahoo's number, whatever lineup produced it. Settlement never reads BeefStarter, never reads Roster. A post-acceptance swap, slot shuffle, or bench promotion is neither locked out nor detected. Fraser's ruling: snapshot both teams' starters at acceptance; a player moved into the starting lineup afterward shouldn't count toward that GM's beef total; slot-to-slot moves don't count as roster moves. This is a new scoring engine, not a filter — no beef-specific scoring path exists. Three questions open before a spec: which nine players, does a never-swapped ruled-out player count as a change, and does the GM see why the app's number differs from Yahoo's.
