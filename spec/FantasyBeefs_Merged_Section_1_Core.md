### 1.1 Purpose

Fantasy Beefs is a peer-to-peer wagering platform that operates as an overlay to
an existing Yahoo Fantasy Football league. Fantasy Beefs does not host leagues,
calculate fantasy scores, or modify league standings. Its purpose is to
facilitate deterministic wagering between General Managers (GMs) using Yahoo
Fantasy Football as the authoritative source of fantasy data.

### 1.2 Core Principles

**CORE-001 — Source of Truth**
Yahoo Fantasy Football SHALL be the exclusive source of truth for league
membership, team ownership, rosters, starting lineups, scoring, matchup results,
stat corrections, and weekly finalization. Fantasy Beefs SHALL NOT modify Yahoo
data.

**CORE-002 — Platform Independence**
Fantasy Beefs SHALL NOT modify league standings, scoring rules, waiver priority,
roster transactions, or playoff qualification. Wagers exist independently of
fantasy league competition.

**CORE-003 — Peer-to-Peer**
Every wager SHALL exist exclusively between participating GMs. Fantasy Beefs
SHALL NEVER become the counterparty to a wager.

**CORE-004 — No House Edge**
All odds SHALL be fair odds generated by the Simulation Engine. Fantasy Beefs
SHALL NOT apply vigorish, commission, or any house advantage.

**CORE-005 — Wager Currency**
BAB is the exclusive wagering currency recognized by Fantasy Beefs. Fantasy
Beefs SHALL NOT process, hold, or settle real-money transactions.

**CORE-006 — Commissioner Authority**
Commissioners MAY configure league settings before the season and approve BAB
Top-Offs. Commissioners SHALL NOT alter wagers, settlements, odds, simulations,
wallet balances (except approved Top-Offs), or protocol outcomes.

**CORE-007 — Cross-Matchup Wagering**
Any eligible GM MAY wager against any other eligible GM within the same league
regardless of the official Yahoo weekly matchup.

**CORE-008 — Determinism**
Every protocol SHALL produce one deterministic outcome from identical inputs. No
protocol SHALL require subjective interpretation.

### 1.3 Definitions

**GM:** A General Manager participating in a Fantasy Beefs league.

**BAB:** The exclusive wagering currency of Fantasy Beefs.

**Wallet:** BAB immediately available for wagering.

**Escrow:** BAB reserved for unresolved wagers and unavailable for new wagers.

**Handshake:** The protocol executed when a Dynamic Challenge is accepted. It
reserves maximum escrow, freezes the simulation model version, records the
initial simulation, and establishes maximum stake and payout.

**Final Lock:** The protocol executed immediately before the earliest scheduled
NFL kickoff involving any participating starting player. Final Lock freezes the
official wager terms.

**The Adjustment:** The deterministic algorithm executed during Final Lock that
calculates the official stake, payout, and escrow refund for a Dynamic
Challenge.

**Push:** A completed wager with no winner. All escrow is refunded.

**Void:** A wager cancelled by protocol. All escrow is refunded.

> **[HYBRID] Added definitions (session overrides):**
>
> **Anchor Stake:** In a Versus Bet, the issuing GM's committed stake. The
> issuer enters only "I'm putting up $X." The opponent's stake is derived from
> fair odds, not entered. (Row 2.)
>
> **Derived Stake:** The opponent's stake, computed as `fairPot × p_opponent`
> where `fairPot = anchor_stake / p_issuer`. Floored to whole BAB cents; the
> uncollected sub-cent residue is never staked and posts nowhere. (Rows 2, 3.)
>
> **Reserve:** BAB held at buy-in in `reserve:{team_id}`, released weekly toward
> the wagerable wallet by the reserve-ceiling formula, remainder held to the
> Championship Pot at season end. (Row 6.)
>
> **Frozen Balance:** BAB in `frozen:{team_id}`, an alternate commissioner-set
> destination for unspent weekly minimum; never releases weekly, returned only
> at final reconciliation. Distinct from Reserve. Choice locked at season
> kickoff. (Row 6.)

### 1.4 Protocol Invariants

**INV-001** — Every accepted wager SHALL terminate as Final, Push, or Void.

**INV-002** — Every movement of BAB SHALL be recorded as a balanced
double-entry Ledger transaction.

**INV-003** — No GM SHALL lose more BAB than was reserved in Escrow.

**INV-004** — Every wager SHALL reference exactly one Fantasy Week.

**INV-005** — Every protocol event that changes state SHALL create an immutable
audit record.

**INV-006** — Yahoo Fantasy Football SHALL remain the authoritative source of
fantasy statistics and final results.

**INV-007** — Historical protocol outcomes SHALL NEVER be modified by future
Specification revisions.
