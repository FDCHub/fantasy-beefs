For Commissioners

Don't replace your league. Build on it.

FantasyStakes builds on the league you already know and love. Keep the history, rivalries and traditions that make your league yours, then add a new layer of weekly competition with real odds, head-to-head challenges and league-wide pool bets.

FantasyStakes also makes the added competition easier to manage by tracking contests, results, credits and league activity for you.

Trusted Scorekeeping

The odds are real. The stakes are fantasy. The ledger keeps score.

FantasyStakes tracks contests, results and credit movements through a highly accurate double-entry ledger, giving your league a trusted record of game activity and where everyone stands.

How Credits Work

Virtual credits. Real competition.

FantasyStakes is played using virtual credits. Credits have no cash value and cannot be purchased, deposited, withdrawn or redeemed.

Where $ amounts appear in FantasyStakes, they represent virtual FantasyStakes credits rather than U.S. dollars.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Does FantasyStakes handle real money?

No. FantasyStakes does not accept deposits, process payments or make payouts. The game is played entirely with virtual credits. Any separate arrangements between league members happen independently of FantasyStakes.

Do I need to leave my existing fantasy league?

No. FantasyStakes is designed as a companion game layer for the fantasy league you already play.

Do I need to download an app?

No App Store download is required for FantasyStakes 1.0. Open FantasyStakes in Safari or Chrome and optionally add it to your Home Screen.
