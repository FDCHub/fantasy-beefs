What FantasyStakes is

FantasyStakes is a virtual-credit game layer that sits on top of a fantasy league you already play. It adds head-to-head FantasyStakes matchups, weekly prop pools, its own season-long standings and a FantasyStakes Championship.

Virtual credits

FantasyStakes is played entirely with virtual credits. Credits have no cash value and cannot be purchased, deposited, withdrawn or redeemed. All $ amounts shown in FantasyStakes represent virtual credits.

FantasyStakes does not accept deposits, process payments or make payouts. There is no house, and FantasyStakes does not take the other side of any competition and does not charge a vig. Any separate arrangements between league members happen independently of FantasyStakes.
