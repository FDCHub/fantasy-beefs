How the FantasyStakes economy works

Four simple buckets. Different jobs.

Your Wallet

Your Wallet starts the season at $0. It is where your net FantasyStakes gains and proceeds accumulate as Matchups and Prop Pools settle. You start at zero because you haven't earned any FantasyStakes proceeds yet.

Yahoo Fantasy Football Pot

This is the traditional league championship economy. FantasyStakes tracks it separately from the optional Sportsbook competition.

FantasyStakes Weekly Play Reserve

This is the separate amount available for optional FantasyStakes Matchups and Prop Pools. CULV Appreciation Society: Ŧ56 per GM. Mr Whiskers Memorial League: Ŧ60 per GM. Unused reserve is returned to the GM at season reconciliation.

FantasyStakes Weekly Minimum

FantasyStakes allocates the Weekly Play Reserve across the regular season so a portion is available for weekly action. CULV Appreciation Society: Ŧ4 per week = 40 chips. Mr Whiskers Memorial League: Ŧ4 per week = 40 chips. This does not mean you are required to make a wager every week. The app handles the weekly accounting automatically.

FantasyStakes Tokens

Tokens are the underlying accounting unit used by the FantasyStakes league economy.

FantasyStakes Chips

Chips are the easy-to-use gameplay denomination shown when you make Matchups and Prop Pool picks. For example, a league may use 10 chips = 1 FantasyStakes Token. Chips do not create extra value — they simply make gameplay easier to read and play.

Simple mental model: Yahoo Fantasy Football Championship Pot = traditional championship. FantasyStakes Championship Pot = the separate FantasyStakes title race. FantasyStakes Weekly Play Reserve = optional action funds. Wallet = your net FantasyStakes proceeds. Chips = the denomination you use to play.

What to expect when you sign in

CULV Appreciation Society: Your draft is complete, so FantasyStakes has the teams it needs to generate sportsbook-style odds and lines. Once your starting lineup is set in Yahoo, you can begin issuing and accepting Matchups and entering Prop Pools as they open.

Mr Whiskers Memorial League: Your league has not drafted yet, so access will be limited until the draft is complete and starting lineups are set. You can still sign in and view available league and setup information, but odds, lines, Matchups and related gameplay will remain limited until the required data is available.

Nothing is wrong if you see limited gameplay before your league is ready. FantasyStakes unlocks more of the experience automatically as the required draft, lineup and projection data becomes available.

Peer-to-peer by design

Real odds. Nobody plays against a house.

FantasyStakes calculates sportsbook-style probabilities and lines from the fantasy competition, but every game is between people in your league.

No house

FantasyStakes is not the counterparty to your competition.

No vig

No sportsbook commission is built into peer-to-peer play.
