What is FantasyStakes

A new championship built inside your fantasy league.

FantasyStakes adds a separate virtual-credit competition alongside the league you already play, with its own standings race and FantasyStakes Championship.

FantasyStakes uses your league’s scoring settings and lineups together with player projections to simulate outcomes and generate calculated moneylines, spreads and over/unders for head-to-head Matchups and league-wide Prop Pools.

Every result moves the larger FantasyStakes race — changing your competitive position, the standings and the championship picture.

Every week is part of a bigger game.

Why play FantasyStakes

More reasons to care about every week.

Your scheduled fantasy matchup is only one part of the week. FantasyStakes lets you challenge rivals you are not scheduled to play, take positions on calculated markets and compete in custom league pools built around that week’s action.

That means more teams matter, more outcomes matter and more league rivalries stay active. A bad start in your regular matchup does not have to mean your week is over.

More competition. More rivalry. More ways to win.

Sportsbook-style action. Fantasy competition.

Familiar mechanics. A very different game.

FantasyStakes uses familiar sportsbook-style mechanics: calculated odds, moneylines, spreads and over/unders. But FantasyStakes is not a real-money sportsbook.

There is no house taking the other side, no vig, no cash wagering, no deposits and no payouts through FantasyStakes. You compete directly with other GMs in your league using virtual credits.

The objective is not to cash out a wager. Your results accumulate inside the FantasyStakes game, shape the standings and drive a separate championship race.

Use virtual credits to compete. Build your position. Chase the championship.

How you compete

Two ways to put your week in play.

FantasyStakes Matchups are one-vs-one. FantasyStakes Prop Pools are one-vs-everyone. Both use virtual credits and feed the larger FantasyStakes game.

Matchups

Go head-to-head with anyone in your league.

Every opponent in your league becomes another matchup opportunity. FantasyStakes generates calculated moneylines, spreads and over/unders from modeled matchup probabilities, creating peer-to-peer odds-based competition between league rivals.

Prop Pools

Go one-vs-everyone within your league.

Every week brings new league-wide FantasyStakes Prop Pools built around the teams, players and storylines in your league. Make your selection, commit virtual credits and compete against the field under the same governed rules.

How the game works

Start equal. Compete every week. Chase the championship.

Every GM starts with the same Season Opening Allocation of virtual credits. Those credits are the game resource you use to compete in FantasyStakes Matchups, Prop Pools and other governed league competition.

As results settle, your FantasyStakes performance changes with them. Every week can move the standings, change the championship picture and create new opportunities to compete.

The FantasyStakes Championship is the title everyone is chasing. At season end, FantasyStakes Reconciliation provides the complete record of how the league-wide virtual-credit economy finished.

Every week matters. Every result can move the race.

Know your game

A few terms make the whole game easier to follow.

Your Wallet is the virtual credits you still have available to use. In Play is the amount currently committed to unresolved action. Your Weekly Minimum keeps everyone participating in qualifying competition.

Standings show the FantasyStakes championship race. FantasyStakes Reconciliation is the final accounting of where the league-wide virtual-credit economy ended after all governed results.

How to play

The basic loop is simple.

  1. 1

    Start with virtual credits.

    Every GM begins from the same configured starting point.

  2. 2

    Choose how to compete.

    Go head-to-head through FantasyStakes Matchups or go one-vs-everyone through FantasyStakes Prop Pools.

  3. 3

    Put credits into action.

    Make your selection and commit virtual credits to the competition.

  4. 4

    Follow the result.

    FantasyStakes tracks the competition as it moves from open to live to settled.

    Watch the race move.

    Settled results change your FantasyStakes performance, standings and championship picture.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is FantasyStakes gambling?

FantasyStakes uses sportsbook-style mechanics such as calculated moneylines, spreads and over/unders, but FantasyStakes itself is played with virtual credits. There is no house, no vig, no cash wagering, no deposits and no payouts through FantasyStakes.

Does FantasyStakes replace my fantasy league?

No. Yahoo remains where you manage your fantasy team. FantasyStakes adds a separate gaming layer using the league, teams, scoring system and weekly schedule you already have.

What am I trying to win?

You are competing in a separate FantasyStakes standings race for the FantasyStakes Championship.

What are virtual credits?

Virtual credits are the game resources used to compete in FantasyStakes. Every GM begins from the same configured starting point. Credits have no cash value and cannot be purchased, deposited, withdrawn or redeemed.

What are FantasyStakes Matchups?

FantasyStakes Matchups are one-vs-one competitions between GMs using calculated moneylines, spreads and over/unders.

What are FantasyStakes Prop Pools?

FantasyStakes Prop Pools are one-vs-everyone competitions built around your league’s teams, players and weekly action.

How do I learn the app itself?

Players and testers use a separate guided walkthrough that introduces the main screens and shows how to make a first FantasyStakes play. The website focuses on understanding the game and why you would want to play it.
