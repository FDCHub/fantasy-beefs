Why there is no form here

This site is deliberately static: no backend, no database, no form handler and nothing that stores what you type. A contact form would need a server to receive it, and this site does not have one — email keeps the site fast, cheap and free of anything that has to be secured.

Before you write

The FAQ answers the most common questions, including how virtual credits work and whether FantasyStakes handles real money. It does not: FantasyStakes does not accept deposits, process payments or make payouts.
